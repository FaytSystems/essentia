First, check the jar you already use

A smart lid only helps when it fits the container. Before ordering, check the jar opening, the lid specification, and the product's compatibility information. Familiar labels such as “mason jar” describe a family of containers; they are not a substitute for confirming the exact fit.

Essentia is a smart humidity-regulating cap designed for mason jars. Its product page explains the planned hardware, controls, and compatibility. If your jar is not clearly covered, ask about your jar before reserving.

Look beyond the humidity number

A humidity reading tells you what a sensor reports. A regulating system adds a target and a way to respond. When comparing products, look for a clear explanation of what the device measures, what you can adjust, and what it is designed to do when conditions change.

Essentia's design pairs a color touch display with selectable presets and a humidity-regulating cap. You can try the preset display demo before deciding whether that style of control suits your routine. The demo illustrates the interface; it does not measure conditions in your own jar.

Choose settings from the product's instructions and the requirements of what you store. One humidity target does not suit every ingredient or material.
